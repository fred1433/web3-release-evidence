          The first journey on your MVP
          
  
          The bench carries over. The wiring depends on how your product is built, which I have not seen.
          
  
          
    
          What carries over
          
    
          
      
          
        
          Ready in the bench
            
          
          • RPC proxy that cuts chosen methods, between your app and its node
            • 
          
          • Test wallet: accounts, refusal 4001, chain switching
            • 
          
          • Full history of the status line, word for word, not only its last state
            • 
          
          • Direct chain and mempool reads, manual mining, snapshots
            • 
          
          • A moving chain: a block every second and before every click, while payments wait
            • 
          
          • Required-checks manifest, the three statuses, JSON report, CI
            • 
        
          
        
          Depends on your product
            
          
          • Contracts, ABIs, and which network you test on
            • 
          
          • Your indexer or backend, and how it reaches the node
            • 
          
          • Sign-in and wallet flow (embedded wallet, SIWE, account abstraction)
            • 
          
          • Compliance steps: KYC or KYB provider, allowlist source, disclosures
            • 
          
          • Which journey matters most for the launch
            • 
        
          
      
          
      
          EVM is an assumption of this demo. A test wallet exercises the app's logic; it does not show that MetaMask or a phone wallet behaves the same way, which needs its own pass.
